A1 Legacy update

1. Add Section admin page now has a dedicated right-side HTML Section uploader.
   - Upload .html/.htm
   - Submit once
   - A new enabled html_section record is created automatically at the end of the page
   - The section key is generated from the filename and made unique automatically
   - This is separate from HTML animation/media uploads

2. Uploaded HTML sections keep their embedded CSS/JavaScript in the sandboxed iframe.
   A viewport/responsive guard and automatic height bridge are added when stored.

3. Stats cards now use a centered wrapping layout.
   - 1 column on phones
   - 2 on small screens
   - 3 on large screens
   - 4 on extra-large screens
   - incomplete final rows are centered (for example 7 cards render 4 + centered 3)

No database migration or seeder is required for these changes.
