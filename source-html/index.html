Frequently asked questions

No fluff, no runaround — just the answers that actually matter for teams ready to automate at scale.

Still have questions? Contact us

Is Neutron difficult to set up?

Not at all. Most teams are running their first automated workflow within a day. We provide pre-built agent templates for common use cases — no technical background required. For teams that want deeper customization, our TypeScript SDK gives full control.

What kind of workflows can Neutron automate?

Neutron handles repetitive, rule-based operations across your tools — things like data routing, document processing, task handoffs, and multi-step approvals. If your team does it manually on a schedule, Neutron can likely automate it.

How does Neutron connect to our existing tools?

Neutron integrates with the most common business tools out of the box — including Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Google Workspace, and more. Enterprise clients also get access to custom API integrations for proprietary systems.

Is our data secure?

Yes. Neutron uses a zero-trust architecture with end-to-end encryption. All agent actions are logged, auditable, and can be exported for compliance. We never store sensitive credentials — only the permissions needed to execute tasks.

Can we host Neutron on our own infrastructure?

Scale plan clients can deploy Neutron Core on their own private cloud (AWS, Azure, or GCP) via Docker. This gives your team full data sovereignty and compliance control.

What happens if an agent makes a mistake?

Every agent operates within guardrails you define. If an action falls outside your set parameters, Neutron pauses and flags it for human review. You stay in control at every step.
